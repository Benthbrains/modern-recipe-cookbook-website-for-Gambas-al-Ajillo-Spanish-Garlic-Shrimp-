Gambas al Ajillo

Spanish Garlic Shrimp

A traditional Spanish dish made with juicy shrimp, garlic, olive oil, paprika and lemon. Perfect as a starter or a light meal!

Preparation: 5 minutes

Cooking: 5 minutes

Total time: 10 minutes

Servings: 4–6 people

Ingredients

  • 1 pound large raw shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • Salt to taste
  • ½ cup extra virgin olive oil
  • 10 garlic cloves, chopped
  • 1 teaspoon red pepper flakes
  • 1 teaspoon sweet paprika
  • 2 tablespoons dry sherry or white wine
  • Juice of ½ lemon
  • ½ cup fresh chopped parsley

How to Prepare

  1. Pat the shrimp dry with paper towels and season with salt.
  2. Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Add chopped garlic and red pepper flakes. Cook for 30–60 seconds without burning the garlic.
  3. Add the shrimp and paprika. Cook for about 3 minutes, turning regularly, until the shrimp are pink and opaque.
  4. Remove the pan from the heat. Add the sherry, lemon juice and fresh parsley. Mix everything together.
  5. Serve warm with crusty bread and enjoy your Spanish garlic shrimp!
